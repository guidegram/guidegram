## 3. Maker’s First Comment (Post immediately upon launch)

```markdown
Hello Product Hunt community! 👋

I'm thrilled to share **Guidegram**, an open-source, truly portable desktop client for Telegram built with React 19, TypeScript, Electron, and @mtcute.

### The Problem We Solved:
If you manage multiple Telegram accounts for work, crypto communities, or customer support, the official desktop client limits you to just 3 accounts. Worse, all accounts share a single global proxy. If one account triggers a rate limit or the proxy drops, all your accounts go down together.

### The Guidegram Solution:
- **Unlimited Accounts**: Add 5, 10, or 20+ identities with an instant vertical dock (`Ctrl+1..9`).
- **Dedicated Proxies**: Bind separate SOCKS5, HTTP, or MTProto tunnels to individual accounts with real-time ping latency testing.
- **Hardware Anti-Fingerprinting**: Emulates 28+ workstation profiles (Dell XPS, ThinkPad, MacBook Pro) with genuine OS build numbers.
- **100% Portable**: No Windows registry modifications. All sessions and configs live in `./data/`. Run it directly from a USB stick.
- **Group Activity Analytics**: Deep in-chat 24-hour heatmaps and contributor leaderboards without adding bots.

Guidegram is completely free and open source under GPLv3.

We’d love to hear your feedback, feature ideas, and questions! 🚀
```
